Evenings for people who already work

The room follows the source. Units stay in the order of the 2025 Part 2 PDF and the Part 1 outline. The plan is not rearranged to sound easier.

Practice sits inside the evening. Ratios, costing, variances, capital budgeting, pricing, and ethics are worked in class, then missed questions go into an error log.

Review is scheduled, not leftover. Part 2 has a revision after financial statements and two consolidation evenings. Part 1 reviews reporting before cost begins, then closes with two consolidation evenings.

Arabic is there when a term needs it. The course is built for Arabic-speaking professionals: clear explanation, regional business examples, and bilingual clarification beside the English syllabus.

Every evening

Friday and Tuesday. The clock time is confirmed when you enroll. Listed dates stay on the calendar, including when a public holiday falls on that day.

The credential, after the classroom

  1. 01

    Pass the examinations

    This cohort prepares both parts: Part 2 first, Part 1 after the pause. Each part ends with consolidation evenings for the error log.

  2. 02

    Meet the education and experience rules

    The CMA also asks for the education and professional experience that apply to your case. Class does not replace those requirements.

  3. 03

    Keep it with continuing education

    After you earn the credential, it stays current through continuing professional education.
